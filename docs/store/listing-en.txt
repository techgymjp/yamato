Battleship Yamato: Ten-Go

Short description (max 80 characters)
Command the battleship Yamato and survive waves of air attacks to reach Okinawa.

Full description
April 7, 1945. As the battleship Yamato heads for Okinawa, swarms of American carrier aircraft descend on her.

You are Yamato's captain. From the anti-aircraft command post at the top of the bridge, fight off planes coming from every direction.
To capsize the giant, the enemy concentrates torpedoes and bombs on her port side.
If she lists 30° to port, Yamato rolls over and sinks.
Can you survive the onslaught and reach Okinawa?

■ THE CAPTAIN'S VIEW
• Look up, look around, look astern — the sky is full of enemy planes
• Dive bombers, low-flying torpedo bombers and strafing fighters
• Tracers rain down like hail as water columns and fireballs erupt around you
• A detailed Yamato: 46 cm triple turrets, tower bridge and raked funnel

■ SHOOT, DODGE, SURVIVE
• Drag to aim and rapid-fire missiles that home in on planes near the crosshair
• Charge the railgun and sink enemy carriers on the horizon in one shot
• Torpedo tracks appear as red dotted lines — turn your bow toward them to dodge
• Burning planes you shoot down may crash into your ship

■ BASED ON THE REAL OPERATION TEN-GO
• The Second Fleet's ring formation (Yamato, Yahagi and destroyers) under low clouds
• Events follow the historical timeline, from the first raid at 12:32 to the loss of Hamakaze and Yahagi
• If Yamato sinks, the camera pulls back to show her capsize and the magazine explosion
• The results screen tells the true story of Yamato's last battle and its human cost

■ FEATURES
• Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Historical
• Original orchestral soundtrack
• Share your result with friends
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

* The railgun and missiles are fictional weapons for this game.
